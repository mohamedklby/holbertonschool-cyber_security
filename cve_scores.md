Organizations use Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures and Common Vulnerability Scoring System scores to prioritize and manage vulnerabilities effectively.

CVEs identify the vulnerability, while CVSS provides a severity score. Together, they help organizations decide which issues to fix first based on risk.

To integrate CVE information into vulnerability management programs, organizations can:

Prioritize remediation: Focus first on High and Critical CVEs to reduce the most serious risks
Automate scanning: Use vulnerability scanners to detect CVEs in systems continuously
Patch management: Apply updates based on CVSS severity and exposure level
Risk-based approach: Combine CVSS scores with context (e.g., internet-facing systems) to prioritize actions
Continuous monitoring: Track new CVEs and update defenses regularly

By using CVEs and CVSS together, organizations can improve decision-making, reduce exposure to threats, and strengthen their overall cybersecurity posture.
