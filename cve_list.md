The Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures assigns CVE IDs through a structured process. When a vulnerability is discovered, it is reported to an authorized organization. A unique CVE identifier is then assigned, and the vulnerability is documented and published.

The CVE List is managed by the MITRE Corporation, which ensures consistency and uniqueness of all CVE entries.

CVE Numbering Authorities (CNAs) are trusted organizations responsible for assigning CVE IDs to vulnerabilities. They review reported issues, assign identifiers, and publish relevant details, helping maintain an organized and standardized vulnerability database.
